# Customer Churn & Retention Analytics Platform

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end customer analytics solution built using SQL, Python, and Power BI. 
It analyzes customer churn behavior, identifies high-risk customer segments, estimates revenue loss, and recommends retention actions.

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and profitability. The company needs to identify why customers are leaving, which segments are at highest risk,
and what actions can reduce churn.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Calculate customer churn and retention KPIs
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Analyze churn by contract type, payment method, tenure, and services
- Estimate revenue loss due to churn
- Build a churn prediction model
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Recommend customer retention actions

## Tools Used

- SQL for data cleaning, KPI calculation, and business analysis
- Python for EDA, churn prediction, and risk scoring
- Power BI for interactive dashboarding and business reporting
- GitHub for project documentation and portfolio presentation

## Dataset

Dataset: IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographics, services subscribed, account information, charges, and churn status.

## Key KPIs

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Active Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Average Tenure
- Average Monthly Charges
- Revenue Lost
- Estimated CLV
- High-Risk Customers
- High-Risk Revenue Exposure

## Key Insights

- Month-to-month contract customers showed higher churn.
- Customers with low tenure had higher churn risk.
- Customers using electronic check payment method showed higher churn.
- Customers without tech support and online security were more likely to churn.
- High monthly charge customers represented a major revenue-risk segment.

## Business Recommendations

- Offer annual contract discounts to month-to-month customers.
- Provide free tech support trials to customers without support.
- Promote automatic payment methods.
- Improve onboarding for customers in the first 12 months.
- Prioritize high-CLV customers for retention campaigns.
